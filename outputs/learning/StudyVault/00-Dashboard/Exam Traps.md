# Exam Traps

#dashboard #exam-traps #xauusd-checklist

> [!warning] Purpose
> These are common mistakes learners make when turning a live setup into a plan.

## XAUUSD Checklist

> [!danger]- Trap: Confusing trigger with invalidation
> - Trigger is what confirms the setup.
> - Invalidation is where the idea is wrong.
> - A learner needs both before studying an entry.
> - [[XAUUSD Pre-Entry Checklist]]

> [!danger]- Trap: Treating the checklist as a signal
> - The checklist structures thinking.
> - It does not tell anyone to enter a trade.
> - It does not promise profit.
> - [[XAUUSD Pre-Entry Checklist]]
