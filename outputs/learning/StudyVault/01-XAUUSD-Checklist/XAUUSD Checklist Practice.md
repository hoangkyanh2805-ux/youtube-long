## Question 1 - Invalidation [recall]
> Which checklist item tells the learner where the setup is wrong?

> [!answer]- Answer
> Invalidation.

---

## Question 2 - Trigger [recall]
> Which checklist item answers "what confirms the setup?"

> [!answer]- Answer
> Trigger.

---

## Question 3 - Missing Box [recall]
> What should the learner do if one checklist box is missing?

> [!answer]- Answer
> Wait or skip. The setup is not ready.

---

## Question 4 - Bias [recall]
> Which item asks what direction the session is favoring?

> [!answer]- Answer
> Bias.

---

## Question 5 - Liquidity [recall]
> Which item asks where price may sweep traders first?

> [!answer]- Answer
> Liquidity.

---

## Question 6 - Live Setup [application]
> A live setup has an entry trigger but no invalidation. What should the learner do?

> [!answer]- Answer
> Wait or skip until invalidation is defined.

---

## Question 7 - Educational Guardrail [application]
> A viewer asks if the checklist guarantees profit. What is the correct response?

> [!answer]- Answer
> No. It is educational and structures setup study; it does not guarantee any outcome.

---

## Question 8 - Trigger vs Invalidation [analysis]
> Compare trigger and invalidation. Why does the learner need both?

> [!answer]- Answer
> Trigger defines confirmation. Invalidation defines where the idea is wrong. Both are needed to avoid copying a setup without a plan.

---

## Question 9 - Checklist Quality [analysis]
> A learner has bias, liquidity, trigger, and risk, but no invalidation. Is the plan complete?

> [!answer]- Answer
> No. Invalidation is required because the learner must know where the idea is wrong before treating the setup as study-ready.
