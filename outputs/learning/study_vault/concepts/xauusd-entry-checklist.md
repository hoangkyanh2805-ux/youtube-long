# XAUUSD Entry Checklist

Status: seed concept.

## Core Idea

Before following or taking an XAUUSD setup, the learner should check bias, liquidity, trigger, invalidation, and risk. If one box is missing, the lesson is to wait rather than force a trade.

## Common Mistakes

- Entering from a live call without knowing invalidation.
- Watching only the entry trigger and ignoring session context.
- Treating a checklist as a profit promise instead of a risk filter.
